Our Story

Technology with purpose.

Queen Touch Technology (QTT) is a next-generation IT solutions company transforming businesses through innovation, intelligence, and technology. We partner with organizations to reimagine their digital future, drive operational excellence, and achieve agility at scale.

Our multidisciplinary expertise spans enterprise software development, cloud and DevOps solutions, cybersecurity and compliance, AI & data analytics, IoT integration, and strategic digital consulting. Each solution we craft is tailored to enhance user experience, optimize workflows, and accelerate sustainable growth.

With a global vision and local roots in Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu, QTT is committed to empowering enterprises with secure, scalable, and smart technology solutions that deliver measurable business impact — alongside the ready-made products it builds and operates itself.

“At Queen Touch Technology, we don’t just implement technology — we enable transformation that drives lasting value.”

— Sathish Kumar S, Founder & CEO
