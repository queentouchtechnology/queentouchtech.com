Truth of Bible is a Bible study and learning platform designed to make the Bible accessible through a large collection of translations and digital study resources.

The platform supports 160+ Bible translations, giving readers the ability to study, compare, and read scripture in the language and version that suits them best.

Truth of Bible is available as a website and as an Android app on Google Play.
