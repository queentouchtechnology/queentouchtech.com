AI Document Search Development

Find information instantly across contracts, reports, and files with AI-powered search — crafted by QTT.

  • Home

  • AI Document Search

As document volumes grow — contracts, reports, policies, emails — finding a specific piece of information buried inside them becomes a real time drain. Keyword search alone often misses what you're actually looking for. AI document search changes that.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build AI-powered document search systems that understand meaning, not just keywords — letting your team ask a question in plain language and get pointed directly to the relevant section across thousands of documents.

Explore our AI Knowledge Base services if you need this search capability packaged as a customer-facing help center.

1. Semantic Search, Not Just Keywords

We build search systems that understand the meaning behind a query, returning relevant results even when exact keywords don't match the document text.

2. Search Across Multiple Document Types

We build indexing pipelines that handle PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, and scanned files, unifying search across your entire document repository.

  • Semantic search that understands meaning, not just keywords.
  • Search across PDFs, spreadsheets, scans, and more.
  • Direct answers with source document references.

3. Direct Answers With Source References

Instead of just returning a list of documents, we build systems that extract and present the specific answer, along with a reference to the source document.

4. Secure, Permission-Aware Search

We design search systems that respect existing document permissions, ensuring users only see results from documents they're authorized to access.

5. Scalable to Large Document Repositories

We architect the search infrastructure to scale efficiently across growing document volumes, keeping search fast as your archive expands.
