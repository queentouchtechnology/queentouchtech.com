AI Data Analytics Dashboard Development

AI-powered dashboards that surface insights and answer questions about your data — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / AI Data Analytics Dashboard Development

Most dashboards show data — they don't explain it. Getting an actual insight often still requires someone who knows how to build the right chart or run the right query. An AI-powered dashboard closes that gap.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build AI-powered analytics dashboards that let your team ask questions about your data in plain language and get instant answers, trends, and visualizations — no SQL or data analyst required for everyday questions.

Explore our AI Document Search services if your team also needs to search unstructured files alongside structured data.

1. Natural Language Data Queries

We build dashboards that let users type questions like "what were sales last quarter by region" and get an instant chart or answer, without needing query language knowledge.

2. Automated Insight Detection

We integrate automated trend and anomaly detection, surfacing significant changes in your data proactively instead of waiting for someone to notice manually.

  • Natural language questions answered with charts and data.
  • Automated detection of trends and anomalies.
  • Connects to your existing databases and business tools.

3. Connects to Your Existing Data Sources

We integrate the dashboard with your existing databases, CRM, ERP, or spreadsheets, consolidating data from multiple sources into one view.

4. Custom Visualizations & Reports

We build visualizations tailored to how your business actually tracks performance, along with scheduled report generation for stakeholders.

5. Role-Based Access to Insights

We design dashboards with role-based access, ensuring each team or individual sees the data and insights relevant to their responsibilities.
