AI Knowledge Base Development

AI-powered knowledge bases that answer questions instantly from your documentation — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / AI Knowledge Base Development

Traditional knowledge bases rely on customers or employees searching through articles manually. An AI-powered knowledge base understands the question and returns a direct, accurate answer instantly.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build AI knowledge bases trained on your documentation, help articles, and internal wikis — allowing customers and employees to ask questions in plain language and get accurate answers immediately, without digging through pages.

Explore our AI Document Search services if you need this same capability applied to internal files and records rather than a help center.

1. Trained on Your Existing Documentation

We build the knowledge base directly from your existing help articles, manuals, and wikis — no need to rewrite content from scratch.

2. Natural Language Question Answering

Users ask questions in plain language and receive direct answers, rather than a list of articles to search through manually.

  • Built from your existing documentation and help articles.
  • Natural language question answering, not just keyword search.
  • Easy updates as your documentation changes.

3. Always Up to Date

We build update workflows so the knowledge base automatically reflects changes to your source documentation, avoiding outdated answers.

4. Internal or Customer-Facing Deployment

Whether for internal employee support or customer-facing help centers, we tailor access controls and design to match the use case.

5. Reduces Repetitive Support Tickets

By answering common questions instantly, the AI knowledge base reduces the volume of repetitive tickets reaching your support team.
