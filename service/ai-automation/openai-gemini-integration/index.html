Custom OpenAI and Google Gemini integrations that add real AI capability to your product — crafted by QTT.

Adding AI to your product means more than calling an API — it means designing prompts, handling context, and building the right workflow around the model's output. Done wrong, AI integration feels bolted on. Done right, it feels native.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we integrate OpenAI and Google Gemini models into your existing applications, websites, or internal tools — building the prompt engineering, data handling, and workflow logic needed to make AI features genuinely useful, not gimmicky.
