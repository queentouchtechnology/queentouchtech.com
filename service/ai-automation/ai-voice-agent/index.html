AI Voice Agent Development

Natural-sounding AI voice agents that handle calls, bookings, and queries automatically — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / AI Voice Agent Development

Phone calls remain one of the highest-friction parts of customer service — long hold times, repetitive questions, limited staff availability. An AI voice agent can answer instantly, any time of day.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build AI voice agents that handle inbound and outbound calls — answering questions, booking appointments, and qualifying leads — using natural-sounding speech and real understanding of caller intent.

Explore our AI Chatbot Development services if you need the same intelligence available over text and web chat.

1. Natural, Human-Like Voice Interaction

We build voice agents using natural speech synthesis and recognition, so conversations feel fluid rather than robotic or scripted.

2. Automated Call Handling & Booking

Our voice agents handle common call types — appointment booking, order status, FAQs — reducing the load on your support team during peak hours.

  • Inbound and outbound call handling with natural speech.
  • Automated appointment booking and reminders.
  • Call transcripts and analytics for every conversation.

3. Intent Recognition & Smart Routing

We build voice agents that recognize caller intent and route calls appropriately — either resolving the query directly or transferring to the right team.

4. Call Analytics & Transcripts

Every call is transcribed and logged, giving you searchable records and analytics on call volume, common queries, and resolution rates.

5. Integration With Your Existing Systems

We connect the voice agent to your CRM, booking system, or database, so it can look up and update real customer information during calls.
