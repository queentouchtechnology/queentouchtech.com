Manually typing data from invoices, forms, and scanned documents into your systems is slow and error-prone. OCR-powered automation extracts that data accurately, in seconds instead of minutes.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build custom OCR and document automation solutions that extract structured data from invoices, receipts, forms, and ID documents — feeding it directly into your existing systems without manual entry.

Explore our n8n Workflow Automation services to route extracted document data automatically into your other business tools.

1. Accurate Data Extraction From Documents

We build OCR pipelines that accurately extract text and structured fields from scanned documents, invoices, and forms, even with varied layouts.

2. Automated Data Validation

We integrate validation rules that check extracted data for accuracy and flag anomalies, reducing errors before data enters your system.

  • Automated extraction from invoices, forms, and receipts.
  • Data validation rules to catch extraction errors.
  • Direct integration with your existing systems and databases.

3. Direct Integration With Your Systems

Extracted data is automatically routed into your accounting software, CRM, or database — eliminating manual re-entry entirely.

4. Support for Multiple Document Types

Whether it's invoices, ID documents, forms, or handwritten notes, we tailor the OCR pipeline to handle the specific document types your business processes.

5. Scalable Batch Processing

We architect the system to handle both single-document uploads and bulk batch processing, scaling with your document volume.
