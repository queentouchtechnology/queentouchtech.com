Home / Services / Hosting Setup

Reliable web hosting is the backbone of every successful website. A properly configured hosting environment ensures your website loads quickly, remains secure, and delivers a seamless experience for your visitors around the clock.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we provide complete Hosting Setup services, including web hosting configuration, server setup, domain connection, SSL certificate installation, website migration, email hosting integration, and ongoing hosting management for businesses of all sizes.

Explore our Zoho Mail Setup services to register your business domain, configure DNS settings, and establish a strong online presence before launching your website.

1. Web Hosting Configuration

We configure your hosting environment to ensure optimal website performance, security, and reliability. Whether you use shared hosting, VPS, cloud hosting, or dedicated servers, we handle the complete setup process.

2. Domain Connection & Website Deployment

Our experts connect your domain to your hosting server, configure DNS records, and deploy your website so it becomes accessible worldwide without downtime or configuration issues.

  • Shared, VPS, cloud, and dedicated hosting setup.
  • Domain connection and DNS configuration.
  • Website deployment and server optimization.
  • SSL certificate installation and security configuration.

3. SSL Installation & Website Security

We install and configure SSL certificates to secure your website, encrypt visitor data, improve SEO rankings, and ensure a safe browsing experience with HTTPS protection.

4. Website Migration & Email Hosting

Moving to a new hosting provider? We perform secure website migrations with minimal downtime and configure professional email hosting to keep your business communication uninterrupted.

5. Ongoing Hosting Support & Management

QTT provides continuous hosting support, including server maintenance, backup management, uptime monitoring, security updates, troubleshooting, and performance optimization to keep your website running smoothly.
