Generic CRM software often forces your business to adapt to its workflow — instead of the other way around. A custom CRM portal is built around exactly how your team sells and manages relationships.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop custom CRM portals that centralize lead management, sales pipelines, follow-ups, and reporting into one system tailored precisely to your business processes — no unnecessary complexity, no paying for features you'll never use.

1. Centralized Lead & Contact Management

We build CRM portals that capture and organize every lead and contact in one place, with clear status tracking — so no enquiry falls through the cracks.

2. Sales Pipeline Tracking

We design visual, easy-to-use sales pipelines that let your team track deals from first contact to close, with custom stages that match your actual sales process.

  • Custom pipeline stages matching your sales process.
  • Automated reminders for follow-ups and pending tasks.
  • Activity logs tracking every interaction with a lead.

3. Task & Follow-Up Automation

We build automated reminders and task assignments so your sales team never misses a follow-up — improving conversion rates through consistent, timely engagement.

4. Reporting & Sales Analytics

We build reporting dashboards that give you real-time visibility into pipeline health, team performance, and conversion rates — helping you make informed sales decisions.

5. Integrated with Your Communication Channels

We connect your CRM portal with email, WhatsApp, and SMS gateways, so all customer communication history stays logged alongside their record — keeping your team fully informed.
