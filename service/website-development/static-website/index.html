Static Pages (5 Page) Website Design & Development

Fast, affordable, and professional 5-page static websites built to establish your business online — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Static Pages (5 Page) Website Design & Development

Not every business needs a complex, feature-heavy website. Sometimes, all you need is a clean, professional 5-page static website that clearly communicates who you are, what you do, and how customers can reach you.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we design and develop affordable, fast-loading static websites tailored for small businesses, startups, and professionals. Our 5-page static website package covers everything you need — Home, About, Services, Gallery/Portfolio, and Contact — built with clean code, SEO best practices, and mobile responsiveness.

Explore our Dynamic Website services to configure secure hosting environments, connect your domain, and prepare your website for reliable online performance.

1. Simple, Professional Design

We design clean, business-appropriate layouts that build trust and clearly present your brand. No unnecessary clutter — just a focused, professional online presence that represents your business well.

2. Fast Loading & Mobile-Friendly

Static websites are inherently fast since there's no database or backend processing involved. We ensure every page loads quickly and looks great on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices alike.

  • Lightweight, clean code for maximum speed.
  • Fully responsive layout across all devices.
  • Minimal hosting and maintenance requirements.

3. SEO-Friendly Structure

Even a simple 5-page website needs to be found on Google. We structure each page with proper headings, meta tags, and keyword-optimized content, giving your business a strong foundation for search visibility.

4. Ideal for Small Businesses & Startups

A 5-page static website is the perfect starting point for businesses that need a professional online presence without the cost and complexity of a large custom platform. It's quick to launch and easy to maintain.

5. Affordable & Quick Turnaround

Because static websites require less development complexity, we're able to deliver your 5-page website faster and at a lower cost — without compromising on design quality or performance.
