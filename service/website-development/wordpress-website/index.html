Millions of businesses trust WordPress to power their online presence — but a generic theme and default setup won't help you stand out or convert. Your WordPress website should be as unique and capable as your business.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we design and develop custom WordPress websites that combine clean, brand-focused design with powerful functionality. From custom themes to plugin integration and WooCommerce stores, we build WordPress websites that are fast, secure, and effortless to manage.

1. Custom Theme Design, Not Generic Templates

We design custom WordPress themes tailored to your brand identity instead of relying on generic, overused templates. Every layout, color, and interaction is built to reflect your business and engage your visitors.

2. Secure & Well-Maintained WordPress Core

WordPress websites are a common target for attacks when left unmaintained. We harden your website with security best practices, regular updates, and protective plugins to keep your site safe from vulnerabilities.

  • Regular core, theme, and plugin updates to close security gaps.
  • Malware scanning and firewall protection built in.
  • Secure login practices and role-based access control.

3. Plugin Integration & Custom Functionality

Whether you need contact forms, booking systems, membership access, or WooCommerce for online sales, we integrate and configure the right plugins — or build custom functionality when off-the-shelf solutions fall short.

4. Easy Content Management for Your Team

We set up WordPress so your team can update pages, blogs, and products without needing a developer. Intuitive page builders and clear content structures put you in full control of your website.

5. SEO & Speed Optimized

A slow WordPress site loses visitors and rankings. We optimize images, caching, and code structure — and configure SEO plugins correctly — so your WordPress website loads fast and ranks well from day one.
