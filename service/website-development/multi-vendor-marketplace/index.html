Multi Vendor Marketplace Development

Scalable marketplace platforms with vendor management, commission tracking, and centralized control — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Multi Vendor Marketplace Development

A multi vendor marketplace is far more complex than a single-store eCommerce website — it needs to manage multiple sellers, commissions, and transactions while keeping the experience seamless for buyers.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop multi vendor marketplace platforms built to handle vendor onboarding, product management, commission structures, and centralized administration — all while delivering a smooth shopping experience for customers.

Explore our eCommerce Website services if a single-store platform fits your business better than a full marketplace.

1. Vendor Onboarding & Dashboards

We build dedicated vendor dashboards where sellers can register, manage their own product listings, track orders, and view earnings — reducing the administrative load on your team.

2. Commission & Payout Management

Marketplace revenue depends on accurate commission handling. We build configurable commission structures and automated payout tracking, so vendor earnings and platform revenue are always transparent.

  • Configurable commission rules per vendor or category.
  • Automated order routing to the correct vendor.
  • Centralized admin panel for managing vendors and disputes.

3. Centralized Admin Control

We build powerful admin panels giving you full oversight of vendors, products, orders, and payouts — with the ability to approve listings, manage disputes, and monitor marketplace performance.

4. Unified Buyer Experience

Even with multiple vendors, buyers should experience one cohesive marketplace. We design unified search, cart, and checkout flows so customers can purchase from multiple sellers in a single, seamless transaction.

5. Built to Scale

Marketplaces grow quickly once traction hits. We architect the platform with scalable infrastructure, so it handles increasing vendors, products, and transaction volume without performance issues.

"A marketplace succeeds when vendors are empowered and buyers are served seamlessly. At QTT, we build platforms that balance both — and scale as your marketplace grows."

— Sathish Kumar S, Founder & CEO, Queen Touch Technology
