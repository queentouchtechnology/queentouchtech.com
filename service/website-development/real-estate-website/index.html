Real Estate Website Design & Development

Property listing websites with powerful search, galleries, and lead capture — built to convert browsers into buyers, crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Real Estate Website Design & Development

Property buyers and renters do almost all of their searching online before ever contacting an agent. If your real estate website doesn't showcase listings clearly and capture interest quickly, you're losing leads to competitors.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we design real estate websites built around searchable property listings, high-quality galleries, and simple enquiry forms. Whether you're an individual agent or a real estate agency, we build websites that turn visitors into qualified leads.

Explore our Dynamic Website services to power your real estate platform with a custom, database-driven backend.

1. Searchable Property Listings

We build property listing pages with filters for location, price, property type, and size — helping visitors quickly narrow down options that match exactly what they're looking for.

2. High-Impact Galleries & Virtual Tours

Photos sell properties. We integrate high-resolution image galleries and, where needed, virtual tour or video embeds, giving buyers and renters a strong sense of each property before they ever visit.

  • Filterable listings by location, budget, and property type.
  • Image galleries and optional virtual tour integration.
  • Map integration showing property locations at a glance.

3. Lead Capture on Every Listing

Every property page is an opportunity. We add contact and enquiry forms directly on listing pages, so interested buyers and renters can reach out without hunting for a contact page.

4. Agent & Agency Profiles

We build dedicated profile pages for agents or agencies, showcasing experience, listings, and client testimonials — helping build trust with prospective buyers and sellers.

5. SEO Optimized for Local Property Searches

We structure your real estate website with location-based keywords and schema markup for property listings, helping you rank higher when buyers search for properties in your area.
