School & College Website Design & Development

Modern, informative education websites built to engage students, parents, and faculty — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / School & College Website Design & Development

For schools and colleges, your website is often the first interaction a parent or prospective student has with your institution. It needs to inform, reassure, and simplify processes like admissions and communication.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we design school and college websites that present your institution professionally while making essential information — admissions, courses, faculty, and events — easy to find. Our education websites are built to serve parents, students, and staff alike.

Explore our LMS Portal services to extend your website with online courses, assignments, and student progress tracking.

1. Clear Admissions & Enquiry Forms

Admissions season shouldn't be a bottleneck. We build streamlined online admission and enquiry forms that make it simple for parents and students to apply or ask questions directly through your website.

2. Faculty, Courses & Department Pages

We structure dedicated pages for faculty profiles, courses, and departments, giving prospective students and parents a clear picture of your academic offerings and the people behind them.

  • Organized course and department listings for easy browsing.
  • Faculty profile pages highlighting qualifications and expertise.
  • Searchable academic calendar and notice board sections.

3. Events, Notices & Gallery Updates

Keeping your community informed matters. We build easy-to-update notice boards, event calendars, and photo galleries so your institution can share achievements, announcements, and campus life without hassle.

4. Mobile-Friendly for Parents on the Go

Most parents check school updates from their phones. We ensure your school or college website is fully responsive, so notices, forms, and information are accessible on any device.

5. SEO Optimized to Reach More Students

We optimize your education website with location-based SEO and keyword-rich content, helping your school or college appear in searches from families looking for institutions like yours.
