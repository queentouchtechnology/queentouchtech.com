School ERP Software Development

Custom School ERP unifying admissions, fees, attendance, and academics — crafted by QTT.

Running a school involves far more than teaching — admissions, fee collection, attendance, exams, and communication all need to work together smoothly. A School ERP brings every administrative process into one connected system.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop custom School ERP software that manages admissions, fee collection, attendance, academics, and parent communication — built around how your institution actually runs, from single schools to multi-branch groups.

Explore our School & College Website services to pair your ERP with a public-facing institutional website.

1. Admissions & Student Records

We build digital admission workflows and centralized student records, replacing paper-based processes with a searchable, secure system accessible to authorized staff.

2. Fee Management & Collection

We integrate fee structuring, online payment collection, and due tracking, giving administrators clear visibility into collections and reducing manual follow-ups.

  • Online fee payment and automated due reminders.
  • Attendance tracking integrated with academic records.
  • Report card and exam result generation.

3. Attendance & Academic Tracking

We connect attendance tracking with academic records, giving teachers and administrators a unified view of student performance and presence throughout the term.

4. Exams, Report Cards & Communication

We build exam management and report card generation tools, along with parent communication features — keeping families informed about academic progress and school updates.

5. Multi-Branch & Role-Based Access

For institutions with multiple branches, we architect the School ERP to manage each campus separately while giving central administrators consolidated oversight — all with role-based access control.
