Inventory Management Software Development

Custom inventory systems with real-time stock tracking, purchase orders, and alerts — crafted by QTT.

  • Home

  • Inventory Management

Stockouts and overstocking both cost money — and both usually trace back to inaccurate, delayed inventory data. Inventory management software gives you real-time visibility into what you have, where it is, and when to reorder.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop custom inventory management software that tracks stock levels, purchase orders, and warehouse movement in real time — built around your specific product categories and supply chain.

Explore our Manufacturing ERP services if you need inventory management combined with full production planning.

1. Real-Time Stock Tracking

We build systems that track stock levels across warehouses or locations in real time, giving you an accurate, always-current view of available inventory.

2. Purchase Order & Supplier Management

We integrate purchase order creation and supplier tracking, streamlining reordering and giving you visibility into supplier performance and delivery timelines.

  • Real-time stock levels across multiple warehouses or locations.
  • Automated low-stock alerts and reorder suggestions.
  • Purchase order tracking from request to delivery.

3. Low-Stock Alerts & Reorder Automation

We build automated alerts that notify your team when stock falls below set thresholds, along with reorder suggestions — helping prevent stockouts before they happen.

4. Barcode & Batch Tracking

We integrate barcode scanning and batch or serial number tracking, giving you precise control over inventory movement, expiry dates, and traceability.

5. Reporting & Inventory Analytics

We build reporting dashboards showing stock movement, turnover rates, and valuation — giving management the data needed to make informed purchasing and stocking decisions.
