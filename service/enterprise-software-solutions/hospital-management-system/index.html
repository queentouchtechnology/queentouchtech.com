Hospital Management System Development

Custom HMS software unifying patient records, billing, and department operations — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Hospital Management System Development

Hospitals run on coordination — between reception, doctors, labs, pharmacy, and billing. When those departments operate on disconnected systems or paper records, delays and errors follow. A Hospital Management System brings it all together.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop custom Hospital Management Systems that unify patient records, appointment scheduling, billing, and department coordination — built around how your hospital or clinic actually operates.

Explore our Hospital Website services to pair your HMS with a patient-facing website and appointment booking.

1. Centralized Patient Records

We build a unified patient record system covering medical history, prescriptions, and visit records — accessible securely across departments for coordinated, informed care.

2. Appointment & Department Coordination

We integrate appointment scheduling and department-wise patient routing, reducing wait times and ensuring smooth handoffs between reception, doctors, and diagnostic departments.

  • Centralized patient records accessible across departments.
  • Appointment scheduling with doctor and department coordination.
  • Integrated billing, insurance, and pharmacy modules.

3. Billing & Insurance Management

We build integrated billing modules covering consultations, procedures, and pharmacy charges, along with insurance claim tracking — reducing billing errors and delays.

4. Pharmacy & Inventory Integration

We connect pharmacy and medical inventory tracking to the HMS, giving administrators real-time visibility into stock levels and reducing shortages or overstocking.

5. Role-Based Access & Data Security

We design the system with strict role-based access, ensuring sensitive patient data is only visible to authorized medical and administrative staff.

"In healthcare, coordination between departments can be the difference in patient care quality. At QTT, we build Hospital Management Systems that keep every department working from the same accurate information."
