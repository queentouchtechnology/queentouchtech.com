Building a SaaS product means solving problems most businesses never face — multi-tenant data isolation, subscription billing, and infrastructure that scales with every new customer. Getting the SaaS platform architecture right from day one matters.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop custom SaaS platforms from the ground up — covering multi-tenant architecture, subscription and billing management, and scalable infrastructure — built to support your product as it grows from first customer to thousands.

Explore our CRM Portal services if you need a single-tenant customer management system rather than a full multi-tenant SaaS product.

1. Multi-Tenant Architecture

We architect your SaaS platform with secure multi-tenant data isolation, ensuring each customer's data stays separate and protected while sharing the same underlying infrastructure efficiently.

2. Subscription & Billing Management

We integrate subscription plans, recurring billing, and usage-based pricing models, giving you the flexibility to monetize your SaaS product the way your business model requires.

  • Multi-tenant data isolation with shared, efficient infrastructure.
  • Recurring billing, plan upgrades, and usage-based pricing support.
  • Admin dashboard for managing tenants, plans, and usage.

3. Scalable Cloud Infrastructure

We build your SaaS platform on cloud infrastructure designed to scale horizontally, so performance stays consistent as your customer base and data volume grow.

4. Admin & Tenant Management Dashboards

We build centralized admin dashboards for managing tenants, subscription plans, and usage analytics, alongside tenant-facing dashboards for your end customers.

5. API-First & Integration Ready

We design your SaaS platform with an API-first approach, making it easy to integrate with third-party tools and enabling future extensibility as your product roadmap evolves.
