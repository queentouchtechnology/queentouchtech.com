Manufacturing ERP Software Development

Custom ERP software unifying production planning, inventory, and quality tracking — crafted by QTT.

  • Home

  • Manufacturing ERP

Manufacturing operations depend on tight coordination between production planning, raw material inventory, and quality control. When these run on disconnected spreadsheets, delays and waste follow. A Manufacturing ERP connects every stage of production.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop custom Manufacturing ERP software covering production planning, raw material and finished goods inventory, and quality tracking — built around your factory's specific processes and output requirements.

Explore our Inventory Management services if you need standalone stock tracking without full production planning.

1. Production Planning & Scheduling

We build production planning tools that schedule manufacturing runs based on demand, machine availability, and material stock — reducing downtime and missed deadlines.

2. Raw Material & Finished Goods Inventory

We integrate inventory tracking for raw materials and finished goods, giving production managers real-time visibility into what's available and what needs reordering.

  • Production scheduling based on demand and material availability.
  • Real-time raw material and finished goods inventory tracking.
  • Quality control checkpoints integrated into production stages.

3. Quality Control & Compliance Tracking

We build quality checkpoints into the production workflow, logging inspection results and compliance data at each stage to maintain consistent product quality.

4. Machine & Resource Utilization

We track machine usage, downtime, and resource allocation, helping management identify bottlenecks and improve overall production efficiency.

5. Reporting & Production Analytics

We build dashboards showing production output, material costs, and efficiency metrics — giving management the data needed to optimize manufacturing operations.
