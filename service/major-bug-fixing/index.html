Resolve critical software issues with QTT's Major Bug Fixing services. We diagnose and fix complex website and application bugs to improve security, stability, performance, and business continuity.

Critical software issues can disrupt business operations, impact customer trust, and lead to costly downtime. Major bugs affecting core functionality, security, or system performance require immediate attention to restore stability and ensure uninterrupted business continuity.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we provide expert Major Bug Fixing Services for websites, web applications, enterprise software, and mobile applications. Our experienced developers identify the root cause of complex issues, implement reliable fixes, and thoroughly test every solution to ensure long-term system stability.

Explore our Minor Bug Fixing services to configure secure hosting environments, connect your domain, and prepare your website for reliable online performance.

1. Critical Issue Diagnosis & Resolution

We perform in-depth analysis to identify the root cause of application failures, crashes, server errors, and unexpected system behavior. Our team delivers permanent solutions rather than temporary fixes.

2. Application Stability & Recovery

Major bugs can affect the reliability of your software. We restore application stability by resolving backend failures, API issues, database errors, and business logic defects that impact day-to-day operations.

  • Resolve critical application crashes and system failures.
  • Fix database, API, authentication, and server-related issues.
  • Improve application reliability and overall system performance.

3. Security & Performance Improvements

We eliminate vulnerabilities, optimize resource usage, and enhance application performance to ensure your software remains secure, responsive, and capable of handling growing business demands.

4. Comprehensive Testing & Validation

Every fix undergoes rigorous testing to verify functionality, compatibility, and performance across different browsers, devices, and environments, minimizing the risk of future issues.

5. Ongoing Technical Support & Maintenance

Beyond resolving critical issues, QTT offers continuous monitoring, maintenance, and technical support to help prevent recurring bugs and keep your applications operating efficiently.
