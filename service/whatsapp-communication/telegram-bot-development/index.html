Telegram's large communities and channels need active management — moderating conversations, answering repeated questions, and sending timely updates. A custom Telegram bot handles all of it automatically.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build custom Telegram bots for community management, customer support, and automated notifications — tailored to whether you're running a public community, a support channel, or an internal team tool.

Explore our WhatsApp Chatbot services if your audience communicates primarily on WhatsApp rather than Telegram.

1. Community Moderation & Management

We build bots that automate community moderation — welcome messages, spam filtering, and rule enforcement — keeping large groups manageable.

2. Automated Support & FAQs

We integrate automated responses for common questions, reducing the manual workload of answering the same queries repeatedly in busy channels.

  • Automated moderation, welcome messages, and spam filtering.
  • FAQ and support automation for community channels.
  • Scheduled announcements and notification broadcasts.

3. Scheduled Announcements & Notifications

We build scheduling tools for announcements, updates, and reminders, ensuring your community stays informed without manual posting.

4. Custom Commands & Integrations

We build custom bot commands tailored to your community's needs, along with integrations to external tools, databases, or APIs as required.

5. Analytics & Engagement Tracking

We build reporting on member activity, engagement, and bot usage, helping you understand how your community interacts with the bot.
