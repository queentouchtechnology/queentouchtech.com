Email Automation Development

Custom email automation for drip campaigns, transactional emails, and follow-ups — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Email Automation Development

Manually sending follow-up emails, onboarding sequences, and transactional receipts doesn't scale. Email automation runs those sequences reliably in the background, based on the triggers you define.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build custom email automation systems covering drip campaigns, onboarding sequences, and transactional emails — integrated with your existing CRM or application so emails send automatically based on real customer actions.

Explore our Bulk SMS Portal services to combine email automation with SMS for multi-channel outreach.

1. Automated Drip Campaigns

We build multi-step email sequences that nurture leads or onboard new customers automatically, triggered by signup, purchase, or specific behaviors.

2. Transactional Email Reliability

We build reliable transactional email delivery for receipts, confirmations, and password resets, ensuring critical emails reach inboxes, not spam folders.

  • Multi-step drip campaigns triggered by customer actions.
  • Reliable transactional email delivery and formatting.
  • Open, click, and conversion tracking for every campaign.

3. Behavior-Triggered Automation

We build automation triggered by specific customer behavior — cart abandonment, inactivity, or milestone events — sending the right message at the right moment.

4. Segmentation & Personalization

We integrate audience segmentation and dynamic personalization, so emails feel relevant to each recipient rather than generic blasts.

5. Analytics & Deliverability Optimization

We build tracking for opens, clicks, and conversions, while optimizing sender reputation and deliverability so your emails consistently reach the inbox.
