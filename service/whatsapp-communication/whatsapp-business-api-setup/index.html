WhatsApp Business API Setup

Complete WhatsApp Business API setup and verification for reliable, professional messaging — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / WhatsApp Business API Setup

The WhatsApp Business API opens up automation, verified branding, and higher messaging limits — but the setup process involves business verification, provider selection, and configuration that's easy to get wrong.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we handle the complete WhatsApp Business API setup process — from business verification and provider selection to number configuration and template approval — so your business can start messaging customers reliably and at scale.

Explore our WhatsApp Chatbot services to add automated conversations on top of your new WhatsApp Business API setup.

1. Business Verification & Setup

We guide your business through Meta's verification process and API provider setup, avoiding the common delays and rejections businesses face going it alone.

2. Number Configuration & Migration

We handle number registration or migration from WhatsApp Business App to the API, ensuring your existing contacts and chat history transition smoothly.

  • Business verification and API provider setup handled end-to-end.
  • Message template creation and approval support.
  • Green tick verification guidance for eligible businesses.

3. Message Template Creation & Approval

We create and submit message templates for approval, ensuring your notifications, order updates, and marketing messages comply with WhatsApp's policies.

4. Integration With Your Systems

We connect your new WhatsApp Business API to your CRM, website, or support tools, enabling automated and manual messaging from one place.

5. Green Tick & Brand Verification Support

For eligible businesses, we support the process of obtaining WhatsApp's official green tick verification, adding credibility to your business profile.
