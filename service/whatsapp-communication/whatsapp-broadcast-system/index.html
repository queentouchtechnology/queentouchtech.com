WhatsApp Broadcast System Development

Compliant bulk WhatsApp messaging for promotions, updates, and reminders — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / WhatsApp Broadcast System Development

Email open rates keep dropping, but WhatsApp messages get read almost immediately. A proper broadcast system lets you reach customers at scale — without violating WhatsApp's messaging policies.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build custom WhatsApp broadcast systems that send bulk promotional messages, order updates, and reminders through the official WhatsApp Business API — fully compliant with opt-in and template requirements.

Explore our WhatsApp Business API Setup services if you haven't yet configured your official WhatsApp Business API access.

1. Compliant Bulk Messaging

We build broadcast systems that follow WhatsApp's official policies around opt-in consent and approved message templates, avoiding number bans and delivery issues.

2. Audience Segmentation

We integrate audience segmentation, letting you target broadcasts by customer group, purchase history, or engagement — instead of blasting every contact the same message.

  • Compliant messaging through approved templates and opt-in lists.
  • Audience segmentation for targeted broadcasts.
  • Delivery and read-rate analytics for every campaign.

3. Scheduled & Triggered Campaigns

We build scheduling tools for planned campaigns, along with trigger-based broadcasts tied to events like abandoned carts or upcoming appointments.

4. Delivery & Engagement Analytics

We build reporting dashboards showing delivery rates, read rates, and response rates, giving you clear visibility into campaign performance.

5. Integration With Your Customer Data

We connect the broadcast system to your CRM or customer database, so messaging lists stay accurate and up to date automatically.
