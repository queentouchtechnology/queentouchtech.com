Flutter Mobile App Development

One codebase, two platforms — cross-platform Flutter apps built fast and maintained easily, crafted by QTT.

  • Home
  • Services
  • Flutter Mobile App

Building and maintaining separate Android and iOS apps doubles your development time and cost. A cross-platform Flutter app gives you both platforms from a single codebase — without compromising on performance.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we develop Flutter mobile apps that deliver a near-native experience on both Android and iOS, while keeping development faster, updates simpler, and long-term maintenance costs lower.

Explore our Custom Business App services if you need an app built around a specific business workflow.

1. One Codebase, Two Platforms

We build your app once in Flutter and deploy it to both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store — cutting development time and ensuring feature parity across platforms.

2. Near-Native Performance & Feel

Flutter's rendering engine delivers smooth animations and fast performance that closely matches native apps, so users get a consistent, high-quality experience on any device.

  • Consistent UI and behavior across Android and iOS.
  • Faster development cycles and quicker time to market.
  • Easier long-term maintenance with a single codebase.

3. Custom UI Tailored to Your Brand

We design custom interfaces in Flutter that reflect your brand identity, rather than relying on generic, templated designs — giving your app a distinct, professional look.

4. Faster Updates & Lower Costs

Since Android and iOS share one codebase, new features and bug fixes are built once and deployed everywhere — reducing ongoing development and maintenance costs significantly.

5. App Store & Play Store Publishing

We manage submission to both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store, handling platform-specific requirements so your app launches smoothly on both.
