Restaurant Order App Development

Custom restaurant ordering apps for dine-in, takeaway, and table-side service — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Restaurant Order App Development

Long queues, order mix-ups, and overworked staff often trace back to one problem — an ordering process that hasn't kept up with customer expectations. A dedicated restaurant order app fixes that.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build restaurant order apps covering dine-in table ordering, takeaway, and QR-code menu ordering — reducing wait times, minimizing order errors, and giving your staff a simpler workflow.

Explore our Food Delivery App services if you also need home delivery with live order tracking.

1. QR Code Table Ordering

We build QR-code based menu and ordering systems, letting customers scan, browse, and order directly from their table — reducing staff workload and speeding up service.

2. Order Management for Kitchen & Staff

We integrate real-time order routing to the kitchen and staff dashboards, ensuring orders are received accurately and prepared without manual re-entry or miscommunication.

  • QR-code menu and table-side ordering.
  • Real-time order routing to kitchen displays.
  • Integrated billing and payment collection.

3. Takeaway & Pre-Ordering

We build takeaway ordering flows that let customers order ahead and pick up without waiting in line, improving turnaround and customer satisfaction during peak hours.

4. Integrated Billing & Payments

We integrate secure payment options directly into the ordering flow, allowing customers to pay via card, UPI, or wallet — with automatic billing and receipt generation.

5. Menu & Inventory Management

We build simple admin tools for updating menu items, pricing, and availability in real time, so your restaurant can adapt quickly without needing developer support.
