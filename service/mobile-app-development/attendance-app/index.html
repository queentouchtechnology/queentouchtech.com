Manual attendance registers are slow, error-prone, and hard to audit. A dedicated attendance app gives you accurate, real-time visibility into who's present, on leave, or running late.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build custom attendance apps for schools, offices, and field teams — combining QR or GPS-based check-in, leave management, and real-time reporting into one reliable system.

Explore our QR Code Attendance System Setup services if you need a ready-to-deploy QR check-in solution.

1. QR & GPS-Based Check-In

We build fast, tamper-resistant check-in methods using QR codes or GPS location verification, ensuring attendance is marked accurately and only from approved locations.

2. Leave & Shift Management

We integrate leave request workflows and shift scheduling directly into the app, so employees or students can apply for leave and check schedules without separate paperwork.

  • QR code or GPS-verified check-in and check-out.
  • Leave requests and approvals handled within the app.
  • Automated late arrival and absence alerts.

3. Real-Time Reporting & Dashboards

We build admin dashboards showing real-time attendance data, trends, and exportable reports — giving managers and administrators instant visibility without manual compilation.

4. Notifications & Alerts

We integrate automated notifications for late check-ins, absences, and pending leave approvals, keeping managers and administrators informed without constant manual checking.

5. Built for Schools, Offices & Field Teams

Whether you're tracking classroom attendance, office staff, or field employees across locations, we tailor the attendance app's features to match how your organization actually operates.
