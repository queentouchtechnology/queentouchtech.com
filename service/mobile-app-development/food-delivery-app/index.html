Food Delivery App Development

Custom food delivery apps with ordering, live tracking, and secure payments — crafted by QTT.

Home / Services / Food Delivery App Development

The food delivery market runs on convenience — customers expect to browse, order, and track their food in just a few taps. Your food delivery app needs to deliver that experience flawlessly.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we build custom food delivery apps covering the full journey: menu browsing, cart and checkout, live order tracking, and delivery management — tailored to how your restaurant or delivery business actually operates.

Explore our Restaurant Order App services if you need in-house ordering rather than a full delivery platform.

1. Menu Browsing & Easy Ordering

We design clean, appetite-friendly menu layouts with categories, images, and customization options, making it simple for customers to browse and place orders quickly.

2. Live Order Tracking

We integrate real-time order tracking so customers can follow their food from confirmation to delivery, reducing support calls and improving customer confidence.

  • Real-time order status updates from preparation to delivery.
  • Secure payment gateway integration for cards, UPI, and wallets.
  • Delivery partner assignment and route tracking.

3. Secure Payment Integration

We integrate reliable payment gateways supporting multiple payment methods, ensuring smooth, secure checkout experiences that keep customers coming back.

4. Delivery & Order Management Dashboard

We build admin dashboards for managing incoming orders, delivery assignments, and menu updates — giving your team full control over daily operations.

5. Push Notifications & Customer Engagement

We integrate push notifications for order updates, offers, and promotions, helping you keep customers engaged and encourage repeat orders.
