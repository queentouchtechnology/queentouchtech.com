Website Backup Setup

Protect your website with secure automated backups, database protection, cloud storage integration, and fast disaster recovery solutions by Queen Touch Technology (QTT).

Home / Services / Website Backup Setup

Your website is one of your most valuable business assets, and protecting it from accidental data loss, cyberattacks, server failures, or human errors is essential. A reliable Website Backup Setup ensures your website files, databases, emails, and configurations are securely backed up and can be restored quickly whenever needed.

At Queen Touch Technology (QTT), we provide professional Website Backup Setup services that include automated backup scheduling, secure cloud storage integration, database backups, and disaster recovery planning. Our solutions help businesses minimize downtime and keep their websites safe and always recoverable.

Explore our DNS Configuration services to configure secure hosting environments, connect your domain, and prepare your website for reliable online performance.

1. Automated Website Backup Configuration

Manual backups are unreliable and time-consuming. We configure automated daily, weekly, or custom backup schedules to ensure your website files and databases are always protected without any manual effort.

2. Secure Database & File Backups

Your website consists of important files, databases, media, and configurations. Our backup solutions securely store every critical component, allowing complete restoration whenever required.

  • Automated website file backups.
  • Secure MySQL and database backups.
  • Backup verification and integrity checks.
  • Multiple backup retention policies.

3. Cloud Backup Integration

We integrate trusted cloud storage providers such as Google Drive, Dropbox, Amazon S3, Microsoft OneDrive, and other secure cloud platforms to keep backup copies protected even if your hosting server experiences failure.

4. Fast Website Recovery & Disaster Recovery

Whether your website is affected by malware, accidental deletion, plugin failure, or server crash, our recovery solutions help restore your website quickly, minimizing downtime and business disruption.

5. Ongoing Backup Monitoring & Maintenance

Backup systems should always be monitored. QTT regularly verifies backup success, storage availability, and recovery readiness to ensure your website remains protected around the clock.
