What It Solves

Most businesses can see what already happened in their data, but not what's likely to happen next. Predictive solutions apply AI-powered analytics and predictive modeling to existing business data — sales, operations, customer activity — so decisions can be made ahead of a trend instead of after it.

Key Capabilities

  • AI-powered data analytics and predictive modeling
  • Analytics dashboards for ongoing visibility into trends
  • Automated data workflows that keep models fed with current data
  • Intelligent automation built on top of the resulting insight

Business Benefits

  • Decisions grounded in data trends rather than guesswork
  • Earlier visibility into emerging risk or opportunity
  • Less manual reporting, more time spent acting on insight

Typical Use Cases

  • Business analytics dashboards
  • Demand and inventory forecasting
  • Lead scoring and qualification

Who It's For

Businesses making decisions from gut feel or scattered spreadsheets who want forecasting, trend visibility, or automated scoring built on their own data — inventory, demand, or lead quality.

FAQs

Do we need a data science team to use predictive analytics?

No — QTT builds the models, dashboards, and automated workflows; your team works with the resulting insight rather than building or maintaining the analytics infrastructure.

What kind of data do you need to build a predictive model?

Existing operational data — sales history, inventory records, lead/CRM data, or similar — is typically enough to start; QTT assesses what's available before scoping a project.

Is this different from a standard reporting dashboard?

Yes — a standard dashboard shows what already happened; predictive solutions add forecasting and automated scoring on top, so the dashboard also indicates what's likely to happen next.
