What It Solves

As businesses grow past spreadsheets and manual coordination, they need a system of record for operations, customers, and inventory. QTT builds ERP and CRM systems and automates the business processes around them, backed by technology consulting to plan the modernization itself.

Key Capabilities

  • Custom ERP software and CRM portals
  • Sector-specific ERP (school, hospital, manufacturing, inventory)
  • HRMS and payroll systems
  • Business process automation
  • Digital strategy and technology consulting

Business Benefits

  • A single system of record instead of scattered spreadsheets
  • Less manual coordination between departments
  • A clear technology roadmap instead of ad-hoc tool adoption

Typical Use Cases

  • Inventory management systems
  • Manufacturing ERP
  • Payroll systems
